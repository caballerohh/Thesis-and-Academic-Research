# Applied Economics & Academic Research

A collection of empirical research projects in capital markets, financial-sector competition and social-policy evaluation, with a primary focus on Peru.

The repository demonstrates the application of econometrics and economic reasoning to policy, market-development and financial-system questions.

## Research Areas

### Institutional Investors and Capital-Market Development

An econometric study of the relationship between institutional investor portfolios, macroeconomic conditions and the development of the Lima Stock Exchange.

## Methods

- Panel-data econometrics
- Fixed-effects models
- Robust and clustered standard errors
- Difference-in-Differences
- Propensity Score Matching
- Concentration and market-power indicators
- Applied policy and regulatory analysis

## Repository Purpose

The objective is to connect quantitative evidence with economic interpretation and policy-relevant conclusions. Each project includes its own methodological summary and supporting deliverables.

---

*Research is presented for academic and professional portfolio purposes. Conclusions remain subject to the assumptions, data quality and identification strategy of each study.*
